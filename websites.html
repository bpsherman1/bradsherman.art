The Food Gallery

2025 · HTML & CSS · TheFoodGallery.art ↗

A redesign of the original UW–Madison project, built as a standalone site on a custom domain. The goal was to strip the food-blog aesthetic down to something closer to a fine-art gallery: minimal color, generous white space, and typography that steps back to let the photographs lead.

02

The Food Gallery Original

2024 · HTML & CSS · UW–Madison · art309.art.wisc.edu ↗

Built for a digital art course at UW–Madison. The brief was open-ended: create a web-based artwork. I treated food photography as fine art, applying museum-style presentation — sparse layout, large images, minimal labels — to something people usually see in recipe feeds.

03

Portfolio

2025 · HTML & CSS

This site. The design brief was self-imposed: present work across five different disciplines — painting, drawing, woodworking, photography, and web — without the site itself competing with any of it. Black nav, white pages, Futura throughout. The hero slideshow on the homepage is driven by mouse position on desktop and touch-drag on mobile.
